# October 2026 teaching-pack audit

Scope: all twelve weeks. Every row below was found by looking at the rendered slides, the rendered Word pages and the diagram PNGs, or by a check that measures the built files; none comes from a pass/fail report alone. Status records what was done and how the fix was confirmed. The final section keeps the earlier Week 5 pilot findings (8 October), which were fixed before this round.

Severity: **Critical** = wrong technical content or a broken diagram; **High** = a defect a student or teacher would notice and could be misled by; **Medium** = clarity or layout problem; **Low** = polish.
