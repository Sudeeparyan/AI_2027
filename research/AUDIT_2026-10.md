# October 2026 teaching-pack audit

Scope: all twelve weeks. Findings come from source reading, visual review of slides, Word pages and diagram PNGs, and targeted checks of the built files or actual helpers. A pass/fail report alone does not establish correctness. Status records what was done and how the fix was confirmed. The earlier Week 5 pilot findings (8 October) remain for history.

Severity: **Critical** = wrong technical content or a broken diagram; **High** = a defect a student or teacher would notice and could be misled by; **Medium** = clarity or layout problem; **Low** = polish.
